04 / BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few useful
things to know.

Who is it for?+

Independent strategy researchers, Python strategy builders, and learners who want a more connected research workflow.

What does the AI assistant do?+

It helps clarify rules and propose code changes. You review the suggestion and choose whether to save it. Using the assistant requires your own supported model connection.

What do I need for a backtest?+

Backtesting requires your connected Fusion local client and available local market data. Contact me to discuss the setup that fits your research.

Are the visuals live product screens?+

This page contains reconstructed walkthroughs based on current user-facing pages and localized product copy. They use illustrative content and do not run strategies.

Do the example reports show investment performance?+

No. Preset example reports use synthetic data. Detailed example analysis is not available for every submitted backtest. This showcase does not demonstrate live trading.
